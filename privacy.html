Privacy Policy

Decipher PMO Solutions — website and mobile apps.

The short version

This product has no server and collects no data. We cannot see what you read, search, build, or download.

What stays on your device

Drafts in progress, your recently viewed items, and (if you add one) your Anthropic API key are stored only in your browser or device's local storage. They never leave your device except as described below, and you can remove them at any time by discarding the draft, removing the key, or clearing site data.

The one outbound connection

If you use the Build feature with your own Anthropic API key, your description and answers are sent directly from your device to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to generate your draft. That request is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. We are not a party to it and receive nothing.

Voice input

On the website, dictation uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In the mobile apps, it uses your device's native speech recognition. Audio is processed for transcription only and is never stored by us.

What we don't do

No analytics, no cookies, no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data — because no data is collected in the first place.

Children

This is a professional reference tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
